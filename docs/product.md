# TruePath AI Product Foundation

TruePath AI begins as a focused MVP: a premium landing experience, ethical product foundation, onboarding placeholder, dashboard placeholders, and backend readiness for future student growth experiences.

## Primary users

### Students

Students ages 10-18 need a calm, encouraging space to learn about themselves without pressure to choose a final identity or career.

### Parents and guardians

Families need language that helps them support a student's strengths, confidence, emotions, and future exploration.

### Schools

Schools need scalable tools for advisory, wellbeing, student development, and future readiness without turning students into labels.

## Initial product surfaces

- Landing page
- Onboarding route
- Student dashboard route
- School dashboard route
- Assessments route
- Login route

These routes are intentionally foundational placeholders until real workflows, privacy decisions, and AI boundaries are validated.

## Experience principles

- Warm before clever
- Clear before comprehensive
- Reflective before predictive
- Encouraging before gamified
- Human agency before automation

## Future product areas

- Consent-aware onboarding
- Age-appropriate reflection journeys
- Strength and learning-style exploration
- Emotional intelligence prompts
- Guardian-facing summaries
- School program dashboards
- AI reflection assistant with safety boundaries
- Privacy and data export controls
