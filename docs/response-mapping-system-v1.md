# Response Mapping System v1 (Conceptual)

**Scope.** Conceptual methodology only. This paper describes **how** raw assessment artifacts—choices, rationales, reflections, situational inventions—might **eventually** be interpreted inside TruePath AI as **hypotheses** about development and exploration—not as diagnoses, destinies, or fixed identities.

**Non-scope.** Numeric scoring formulas, thresholds expressed as calibrated probability models, model prompts, retrieval pipelines, database schemas, persistence, analytics, authentication, counselor workflow software, UI copy changes, automation of final judgments—all **out of scope** for **v1**.

**Relationship to sibling docs.** Companion documents such as [`truepath-intelligence-framework.md`](./truepath-intelligence-framework.md) (constructs), [`question-bank-v1.md`](./question-bank-v1.md) (item designs), ethics and safety collateral in `docs/ethics.md`, [`consent-framework.md`](./consent-framework.md), and [`student-safety.md`](./student-safety.md) remain authoritative for policy.

---

## 1. Purpose of response mapping

**Intent.** Translate situated learner expressions into **bounded observational hypotheses** usable for exploratory guidance:

- Signals that invite **reflection** by the learner, guardian, educator, or counselor—not verdicts rendered silently.
- Suggestions framed as **“worth testing”** hypotheses about environments and learning modalities.
- Temporal snapshots that may **combine** across sessions toward **pattern coherence** narratives without permanence guarantees.

Mapping exists to **narrow the search space of supportive next steps**, not eliminate life optionality.

---

## 2. Why single answers must never create fixed conclusions

A solitary response merges **instrument noise**, transient affect, misunderstandings (language accessibility, ambiguity), culturally divergent pragmatic defaults, motivational peaks or troughs, and social masking.

**Operational rule (conceptual):** Treat any single keyed response—especially forced-choice—as **prior weight ≤ anecdote** absent corroborating evidence tiers (see §3). Final narrative outputs must withhold strong attributions until multi-source convergence exists.

---

## 3. Pattern accumulation vs isolated answers

Interpretation distinguishes **aggregation layers conceptually**:

| Layer (conceptual) | Description | Typical interpretive stance |
|--------------------|-------------|------------------------------|
| **Episodic point** | One item at one sitting | Record only—not narratively emphasized alone |
| **Within-session coherence** | Correlated echoes same sitting | Mild reinforcement if non-redundant modalities |
| **Cross-session recurrence** | Similar signals weeks apart differing contexts/stems | Emerging tendency language permitted cautiously |
| **Cross-modality consonance** | Scenario + reflective depth alignment | Confidence eligible to rise—not peak |
| **External corroboration** (later optional human channels) | Educator observational vignette keyed separately | Highest narrative confidence band still hedged |

**Principle.** Strength claims scale with **independent corroborations**, never with rhetorical amplification.

---

## 4. Confidence and ambiguity handling

**Confidence bands (qualitative—not numeric here).**

- **Low:** Single-modality sparse evidence—describe as exploratory only.
- **Moderate:** Two partial convergences or one strong reflection scaffolded by orthogonal behavior-style item—not definitive.
- **High (bounded):** Recurrent cross-session + cross-format alignment—still disallow destiny / clinical / ranking claims.

**Ambiguity etiquette.** Prefer transparent dual-reading statements (“Interpretation uncertain—alternate plausible reading: …”) over silent collapsing.

---

## 5. Contradictory response handling

Contradiction is developmental gold—not failure indicators: identity experimentation, contextual adaptation, masking for safety, growth spurts reversing earlier strategies.

**Process outline (conceptual only).**

1. **Flag tension** internally as `unresolved_polarity`.
2. **Suppress single-pole narration** prioritizing louder recent answer.
3. **Offer narrative fork** emphasizing situational contingency or phase shift.
4. **Invite reflective reconciliation** prompts (later optional cycles).
5. **Escalate** if tension reveals safeguarding content (see companion safety docs).

---

## 6. Cross-dimensional overlap

Dimensions (e.g., curiosity, collaboration, systems thinking) are **non-orthogonal** in lived cognition. A response may **weakly load** multiple constructs.

**Rule.** Attribute **primary** and **secondary** tentative tags with explicit overlap caveat; never treat dimensions as isolated silo scores.

**Example tension:** Strong systems framing of conflict may co-signal analytical thinking *and* empathy depending on language focus—disambiguation requires additional items, not forced disjunction.

---

## 7. Age-sensitive interpretation rules

| Phase | Emphasis adjustment | Language guard |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| 10–12 Discovery | Breadth, playful reversals normal | Avoid occupational anchoring; minimize comparative peer placement |
| 13–15 Potential Awareness | Identity flux expect contradictions | Avoid crystallizing archetypes; spotlight reversible experiments |
| 16–18 Future Direction | Permissible richer ecosystem bridging | Still forbid fatalism; separate aspiration from obligation |

Age shifts **prioritize** which environmental suggestions surface first—not which human worth labels appear.

---

## 8. Short-term vs long-term signals

| Horizon | Examples | Usage |
|---------|----------|-------|
| **Short-term** | Fatigue day, friendship spat week, testing anxiety spike | Modulate confidence downward; avoid trait language |
| **Medium arc** | New strategy adoption month-scale | Tentative competency emergence language |
| **Long arc** months+ | Repeated strategy reuse across differing stressors | “Emerging inclination” permissible—still non-permanent |

Longitudinal narration must avoid **illusory continuity** fabricated from sparse datapoints—silence humility > false trajectory romance.

---

## 9. Reflection weighting vs scenario weighting

| Source | Potential strength | Risks mitigated by pairing |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------------|
| **Forced scenario choice** | Standardized contextual comparability | Social desirability bias; narrow option geometry |
| **Open reflection** | Rich phenomenological fidelity | Verbosity imbalance; linguistic privilege; evaluator fatigue downstream |

Neither modality alone deserves interpretive dictatorship.**Conceptual blending:** Scenario constrains unrealistic self-mythologies; reflections nuance granularity. Divergence triggers moderate confidence reduction + dual narrative.

---

## 10. Observable tendencies vs identity claims

| Approved conceptual label family | Forbidden leap |
|----------------------------------|----------------|
| Observable tendency phrase (“signals emerging comfort with iterative planning under constraint”) | Identity nouns locking essence (“Planner type,” “Natural empath”) |
| Situational behavior (“often chooses clarifying question path in ambiguous vignettes lately”) | Moral attribution (“respectful personality core”) |
| Exploratory preference (“drawn toward collaborative scaffolding in recent reflections”) | Stability prophecy (“future must be teamwork role”) |

**Voice test:** If removing temporal qualifiers breaks truth, the statement is too strong.

---

## 11. Exploration cluster generation

**Clusters** are **fuzzy suggestion bundles**—not taxonomic bins each learner belongs to exclusively.

**Conceptual generation recipe (no math):**

1. Collect tagged hypotheses meeting minimum corroboration (§3 qualitative).
2. **Cluster affinity** loosely by thematic adjacency—e.g., reflective analytical + systems curiosity → exploratory research studio environments (illustrative only).
3. Attach **alternate branch** sibling cluster to preserve anti-essentialism.
4. Populate each cluster card with:

   - **Supporting evidence summary** transparent (which response families).
   - **Counter-indicator caveats**.
   - **Low-stakes exploratory actions** micro (club visit, journaling probe, mentorship chat).
   - **Departure clause** reaffirming permutable identity of cluster membership.

Clusters rotate labels **ephemeral** (“Useful provisional lens Alpha”) rather than immortal branding.

---

## 12. Safe language transformation rules

1. Temporal hedges default: “lately,” “in recent reflections,” “in these contexts.”  
2. Replace absolutes (“always/never/you are”) → observed ranges.  
3. Swap trait nouns → **process verbs** (“tends to revisit assumptions after feedback”).  
4. Offer **dual readings** when ambiguity material.  
5. Ban comparative superlatives vs unspecified peer populations.  
6. Remove occupational fatalism even if user volunteers dream job—reflect aspiration without closure.  
7. Insert **unknown acknowledgment** where evidence thin.  
8. Localize idioms later; avoid culture-sticky metaphors in base English layer.  
9. Neurodiversity: describe **adjustable affordances** without inferring diagnosis.  
10. Socioeconomic neutrality: never praise frugality as virtue or spending as vice.

---

## 13. Confidence thresholds

Even absent numeric scoring, define **qualitative gates** before certain narrative moves:

| Narrative move | Minimal conceptual gate sketch |
|----------------|----------------------------------|
| Mention single dimension by name aloud to youth | Moderate corroboration or high-salience reflective passage |
| Suggest exploratory environment cluster | At least partial cross-item convergence OR robust reflection + weak scenario echo |
| Deprioritize a dimension explicitly | Repeated absence—not one missed item |
| Surface tension / polarity fork | Explicit contradiction OR evenly split recurring pattern |

Thresholds escalate **humility** upward when safeguarding or identity fragility flagged.

---

## 14. Human review / counselor review concepts

Separate **automation-assisted drafting** from **trusted human affirmation** selectively:

| Tier | Intended reviewer | Goal |
|------|-------------------|------|
| **A / Routine** | None if language meets safe rules + confidence low-generic | Routine exploratory scaffolding |
| **B / Sensitive** | Trained facilitator / counselor flagged domain | Emotional intensity, safeguarding adjacent content |
| **C / Institutional** | Designated safeguarding lead | Disclosures imminent harm hints |

Reviews focus on language safety, coherence proportionality—not on “approving correctness of soul.” Maintain audit concepts (who/when rationale) deferring implementation.

---

## 15. Ethical safeguards against deterministic interpretation

1. No ranking humans for punitive allocation quietly.  
2. No covert personality permanence embeddings in marketing personalization.  
3. No discriminatory linkage to protected characteristics absent fairness audit—not modeled here quantitatively.  
4. No silent drift of interpretive thresholds version-to-version undisclosed publicly.  
5. Right to explanatory narrative anchored in surfaced inputs—with redaction safeguarding others’ privacy.  
6. Periodic re-validation that scenario option geometry not privileging culturally dominant pragmatic defaults.

## Worked conceptual examples

### Example α — Collaboration vs autonomy tension

**Question (abbreviated stem):** Poster redo scenario cooperative vs solo option (Discovery phase).  

**Learner response:** Chooses solitary redo path rationale “less arguing.”  

**Possible observations:**

- Emerging preference currently for lowering interpersonal friction cost in creative salvage tasks.  
- Potential parallel collaborative fatigue signal **or** pragmatic efficiency emphasis—ambiguous.

**Prohibited conclusions:**

- Labels “anti-collaborative personality.”  
- Career predictions (“won’t thrive in teams”).  
- Quiet student equals disengagement.

Follow-up corroboration would seek **alternate collaborative contexts** succeeding or reflective elaboration nuanced.

---

### Example β — Resilience narration vs behavior choice divergence

**Question style:** Reflection prompt recounting clumsy→smooth skill trajectory (Potential Awareness).  

**Reflective excerpt (paraphrased):** “I stopped trying after two fails then returned third week bolder.”  

**Parallel scenario keyed same session:** Rapid replan adaptation under abruptly shortened timeline.  

**Possible observations:**

- Oscillation between recuperation hiatus and adaptable cognitive pivoting—moderate coherence around **non-linear recovery arcs**.  

**Prohibited conclusions:**

- Stable grit scalar.  
- Pathologizing hiatus as avoidance disorder.

Narrative should honor **dual rhythm**.

---

### Example γ — Systems thinking lexical density

**Situational response:** Draft skeptic question municipal funding tradeoff harming marginalized youth cluster.

**Signals conceptually:**

- Emerging structural skepticism bridging empathy + analytical inquiry—in single expression.

**Must NOT:**

- Infer activist identity commitment or political affiliation classification.

Confidence moderate but **narrow**—scenario highly scaffolded textual channel advantaging verbally fluent responders; append accessibility caveat conceptual.

---

### Example δ — Social energy exploratory preference emergence

Across **three spaced sessions**:

- Preference items lean toward restorative solitude after taxing tasks consistent.  
- Yet collaboration scenario selects facilitator role twice.

**Mapping posture:**

- Tentative hyphenated tendency: **“Alternating facilitation energy with solitude restoration windows.”** Offer environment suggestions including roles with explicit cooldown affordances—not binary intro/extro tagging.

## How heterogeneous evidence eventually feeds guidance (sans labels fixation)

Contributors:

| Contributor | Contribution style |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Multiple answers** | Weak evidence lattice until redundancy reduces noise |
| **Repeated patterns** | Raises allowable strength of tentative language |
| **Consistency across differing contexts/stems** | Reduces modality-specific bias interpretations |
| **Emotional reflections** | Deepens situational qualifiers; safeguarding hooks |
| **Exploratory preferences** | Seeds environment / ecosystem hypothesis cards |

Feeds (conceptually—not algorithmically codified yet):

| Output family | Illustrative characterization |
|---------------|-------------------------------|
| **Emerging strengths** | Verb-process clusters (“ iterative reframing evident across reflections + adaptations”) |
| **Environments to explore** | Non-exclusive micro trials (clubs, mentorship shapes, modality mixes) |
| **Learning preferences** | Hypothesis tags (“may benefit scaffold mixing narrative analogies + step breakdowns”—not locked style dogma) |
| **Pathway ecosystems** | Bundled exploratory domains bridging education / community—not single occupation mandates |

Permanent labels avoided via **cyclical rebranding prompts** internally (“prior cluster pause—re-measure openness” conceptual).
