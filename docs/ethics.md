# TruePath AI Ethics

TruePath AI uses technology to support self-awareness, not to decide who a student is.

## Ethical principles

### 1. No fixed labels

Students are not types, scores, colors, animals, rankings, or predicted outcomes. Any insight must be written as an invitation to reflect, not a conclusion.

### 2. Exploration over prediction

The platform should help students explore many possible futures. It must not frame career direction as a single correct answer.

### 3. Student agency

Students should understand that they can change, grow, disagree, revisit answers, and hold multiple interests at once.

### 4. Age-aware design

Students ages 10-18 require careful language, privacy boundaries, emotionally safe flows, and responsible adult context.

### 5. AI humility

AI guidance must be explainable, provisional, and transparent. The system should never imply that AI knows a student's identity better than the student does.

### 6. Supportive guardian and school context

Parents and schools should receive language that helps them support students. They should not receive deterministic profiles that could restrict opportunity.

## Red lines

TruePath AI should reject product ideas that:

- Rank student worth
- Predict lifetime outcomes
- Recommend high-stakes decisions without human review
- Encourage surveillance or comparison
- Use emotionally manipulative growth loops
- Hide AI involvement in sensitive feedback

## Ethical product standard

If an experience would make a student feel boxed in, judged, or reduced, it does not belong in TruePath AI.
